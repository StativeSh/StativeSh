# WordRing
A ring of giant uppercase words on a 3D cylinder, separated by red marker crosses, that turns slowly and with scroll.

**Use** once per page as a pause between the hero and the work, on a ruled band. Six or seven verbs that describe what you do.

**The consumer provides** the words: `StudioInk.ring(el, words, {count, radius})` on the `.si-ring-in` element. Use about 16 items and radius 980 on desktop, 12 and 420 on phones. With reduced motion the ring stays still.
