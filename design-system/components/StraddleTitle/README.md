# StraddleTitle
A section title that sits across the edge between a bone band and the ink band below it, top half on bone, bottom half on ink, with a marker note on top.

**Use** to open a major section (projects, about). It is the signature move of the system; use it two or three times per page at most.

**The consumer provides** the title text and an optional scrawl. The element is `.si-straddle` wrapping a `.si-mega`; the split line is computed from the font size, so the title must be one line at its size.

Title colour `red` or `tan`; the scrawl on it is `.si-scrawl--ink`.
