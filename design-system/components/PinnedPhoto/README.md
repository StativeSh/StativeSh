# PinnedPhoto
A small image or GIF shown as a bone-framed photo, taped at both top corners, with a marker caption on the frame.

**Use** when the media is too small to fill a `TornPanel` (a low-resolution GIF or screenshot): pin it inside the panel instead of stretching it.

**The consumer provides** the `<img>` and a short caption in `.si-cap` ("fig. 3: two slits").

Frame 9px with a 26px bottom, rotated -2.5deg, slab `shadow-photo`; tapes `pink` and `tan`.
