# Card
A square, painted card on ink with a hard `deep` slab, for secondary projects and links.

**Use** for things that are smaller than a full project: work in progress, an archive link. Lay two side by side.

**The consumer provides** a label (`.si-label`), a title (`<h3>`, coloured `lavender` or `sand`), one sentence and a display-type link line. Cards may carry a scrawl ("in progress").

On hover, cards tilt toward the cursor and the slab turns `red` (the site does this in its own script; static here).
