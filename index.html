Atoms

An interactive chemistry lab. It started as a 3D viewer for atomic orbitals built from real hydrogen-like wavefunctions, and grew into a toolkit covering every element from H to Og.

  • 3D s, p, d, f orbitals
  • Periodic table
  • Crystal structures
  • Reaction lab

  • Spectroscopy & titration
